# One More — visual direction

Reviewed September 14, 2026. A training journal with the restraint of an editorial website and the clarity of a working tool.

## References

- [Ornn](https://ornn.com/): architectural composition, fine rules, and precise numeric hierarchy.
- [Modal](https://modal.com/): a tightly limited palette and one recognizable green accent.
- [AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/): paper tones, generous space, and a restrained editorial rhythm.
- [Etched](https://www.etched.com/): technical illustration, grid structure, and hardware-inspired details.
- [Linear](https://linear.app/): clear product hierarchy and deliberate typography.
- [Granola](https://www.granola.ai/): warm simplicity and a tangible central object.
- [Cognition](https://cognition.com/): sparse ruled layouts, numbering, and confident whitespace.
- [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/): a single distinctive brand visual and concise actions.

These are design references, not reused templates or assets. The wordmark and SVG weight-plate illustration were created for One More.

## Applied to the product

Paper and graphite surfaces, a muted lime accent, Geist type with IBM Plex Mono for measurements, fine dividing rules, and a horizontal numbered navigation replace the previous card-heavy dashboard. The workout form stays visually direct. A technical weight-plate drawing supplies the identity without decorative gradients or floating interface cards.

On phones, navigation moves to the bottom and movements scroll horizontally. The active-session header contracts and its illustration disappears on phones to give logging controls more space. History, progress, dialogs, and empty states share the same design language.

Google Fonts are optional network-loaded assets; system sans-serif and monospace fallbacks remain available. This is a responsive web app, not a native phone app.
